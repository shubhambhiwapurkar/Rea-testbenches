How to stay alive

  • Follow the wands. The marked route avoids hidden crevasses, and its fixed ropes catch falls.
  • Lean into it. A gust, a side slope or a lopsided pack pulls the white dot off centre. Counter it with Q/E or brace.
  • Watch SpO₂. Above Camp 3 most climbers need bottled oxygen. A bottle lasts about 9 hours at 2 L/min.
  • Mind your hands. Wind chill freezes fingers in under an hour. Stopping makes you colder; so does sweat-soaked clothing.
  • Read the forecast. Summit winds above 15 m/s end climbs. Wait in a tent for a window.
  • Set a turnaround time. The summit is halfway. Most deaths happen on the way down.
